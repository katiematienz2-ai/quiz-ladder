Privacy

Quiz Ladder stores your climber name, best rung, and pass flag in this browser’s local storage. That data stays on your device unless you later connect an account system.

If you subscribe, payment details are handled by Stripe, not by this page. Stripe’s own privacy policy covers card data.

If ads are added to the free climb, the ad network may use cookies. The Climber Pass tier is meant to run without ads.

Contact the site owner through the email you publish with the domain before you apply for ads or payments.
