About

A ladder, not a quizlet.

Quiz Ladder is a single-player trivia game in the browser. The name is the rule: you are on a ladder. The summit is rung 100. Most people will not get there in one sitting.

Why it is hard to finish

Early rungs are ordinary knowledge. Later rungs move into history, science, geography, and logic that punish a guess. The timer starts near 24 seconds and falls toward 9. A miss costs a life and four rungs. Three misses end a free climb.

Why skip is locked

The skip is the paid action. It is visible so the offer is clear, and it does not work until Climber Pass is on. A pass also keeps a checkpoint every 20 rungs, which is the difference between losing an hour and losing the whole climb.

Who it is for

People who want a long quiz, not a score after ten questions. Pub-quiz regulars, students burning time, and anyone who will pay a few dollars not to get stuck on one brutal rung.
